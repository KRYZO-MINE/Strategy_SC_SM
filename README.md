## 5. Budget and purchasing policy

Budget is **flexible but currently unknown**. Spend only when there is a specific need, an expected benefit and a named approver.

### Spend in this order

1. **Use existing resources first:** existing phone/camera, logos, photos, office footage, current software subscriptions and available design tools.
2. **Free/low-cost production:** make a few simple posts and short videos to validate the message before buying tools.
3. **Buy only for a defined gap:** for example, a design/video tool subscription, stock assets, a landing page/form, printing or professional support if the current setup cannot do the task well.
4. **Run a capped paid test:** test one clear offer and conversion objective per brand. Separate budgets and lead tracking.
5. **Scale only after evidence:** increase spend only when leads are genuinely qualified, the company follows up in time, and the cost per result is acceptable to the company.

### Before any expense, record

- [ ] What problem does the purchase solve?
- [ ] Is there a free/current-tool alternative?
- [ ] Is it a one-time cost or recurring subscription?
- [ ] What is the total cost including tax/fees and renewal date?
- [ ] Who will use it, and who will own the account/files?
- [ ] What result should it improve?
- [ ] Who approved the purchase and spending cap?
- [ ] How will the team decide whether to renew/cancel?

### Recommended approval gates

- **Gate A â€” No-cost preparation:** briefs, offer ideas, calendar, copy drafts, checklists and basic organic-content planning.
- **Gate B â€” Production expense:** a paid AI/design/video tool or vendor only when the need is demonstrated and the owner approves.
- **Gate C â€” Ad test:** company-approved cap, duration, objective and lead-handling process before launch.
- **Gate D â€” Scale:** additional spending only after review of qualified leads and business conversions.

Do not buy multiple AI subscriptions upfront. Start with tools already available; trial or pay for one tool only when a specific deliverable needs it. Check renewal/cancellation terms and avoid overlapping tools.
